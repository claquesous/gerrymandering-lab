Congressional District Rule Scorecard

How today's House districts would fare under two simple anti-gerrymandering rules. Districts are scored as drawn; no new maps are proposed.

County ruleA district may include at most one partial county, meaning a county whose land is split with another district.
Line ruleA straight line between any two points in a district may not cross two or more other districts in the same state. Water and other states don't count.
